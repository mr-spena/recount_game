🪙 Tantangan Peti Harta: Regular (+ed) vs Irregular Verb (Berubah Bentuk)

Cara Bermain (Touchscreen / Mouse): Geser (Drag) koin emas ke Peti yang tepat, ATAU sentuh koin lalu sentuh Peti Harta!

👇 KOIN KATA KERJA (SENTUH ATAU GESER KOIN INI): Terisi: 0/8
🧰 Peti 1: REGULAR VERBS

Kata Kerja Beraturan (+ed / +d)

Contoh: walk ➔ walked, love ➔ loved (Sentuh peti ini untuk menaruh koin yang dipilih)

👑 Peti 2: IRREGULAR VERBS

Kata Kerja Tak Beraturan (Unik)

Contoh: go ➔ went, eat ➔ ate (Sentuh peti ini untuk menaruh koin yang dipilih)

⚓ Rumus Penting Kapten: Kalimat Positif (+), Negatif (-), dan Tanya (?)

➕ POSITIF (+) • Pakai Verb 2
S + Verb 2
I went to the island.
➖ NEGATIF (-) • Kembali ke Verb 1!
S + didn't + Verb 1
I didn't go to the island.
❓ TANYA (?) • Kembali ke Verb 1!
Did + S + Verb 1?
Did you go to the island?

PULAU 2 • MATERI INTERAKTIF

📜 Gulungan Harta Karun: Recount Text (Menceritakan Pengalaman)

🏝️ Apa itu Recount Text? Teks dalam Bahasa Inggris yang menceritakan kembali pengalaman atau kejadian di masa lalu secara berurutan. Tujuannya untuk memberi informasi atau menghibur pembaca.

1. ORIENTATION (Pembuka)

Pengenalan: Who (Siapa), Where (Di mana), dan When (Kapan kejadian itu terjadi).

2. EVENTS (Urutan Kejadian)

Rangkaian peristiwa berurutan memakai kata penghubung: First, Then, After that, Next.

3. RE-ORIENTATION (Penutup)

Kesimpulan atau perasaan penulis di akhir cerita: Finally, I was very happy!

📖 My Trip to Coral Island (Liburanku ke Pulau Karang)

Orientation (Siapa, Kapan, Di mana)

Last Sunday, I went to Coral Island with my crew.

🇮🇩 Minggu lalu, aku pergi ke Pulau Karang bersama kru kapalku.

Events (Urutan Kejadian: First, Then, After that)

First, we sailed on our wooden ship in the morning. Then, we swam in the blue sea and saw colorful fish. After that, we cooked grilled fish on the beach.

🇮🇩 Pertama, kami berlayar di kapal kayu pagi hari. Kemudian, kami berenang di laut biru dan melihat ikan warna-warni. Setelah itu, kami memasak ikan bakar di pantai.

Re-orientation (Penutup & Perasaan)

Finally, we went home in the afternoon. We felt very happy!

🇮🇩 Akhirnya, kami pulang di sore hari. Kami merasa sangat senang!

📜 TEKS RECOUNT A1

Riko's Visit to the Sea Museum

[Orientation] Last Saturday, Riko and his sister visited the Sea Museum in Surabaya.

[Events] First, they bought two tickets at 9 o'clock. Then, they saw a giant whale skeleton and many cute turtles. Riko took five photos with his camera. After that, they ate fried rice and drank orange juice for lunch.

[Re-orientation] Finally, they went home by bus at 2 o'clock. They were very happy.

💡 Kosakata A1: visited (mengunjungi), bought (membeli), whale skeleton (kerangka paus), turtles (penyu), took photos (mengambil foto), fried rice (nasi goreng).

📖 Kamus Saku Bajak Laut (Kosakata Bahasa Inggris Level A1)

⏰ Keterangan Waktu Masa Lalu (Time Signals):
• yesterday = kemarin
• last Sunday = Minggu lalu
• last week = minggu lalu
• last holiday = liburan lalu
• two days ago = 2 hari lalu
• this morning = tadi pagi
🛠️ Regular Verbs (Kata Kerja Beraturan +ed):
• walk ➔ walked (berjalan)
• play ➔ played (bermain)
• cook ➔ cooked (memasak)
• visit ➔ visited (mengunjungi)
• watch ➔ watched (menonton)
• help ➔ helped (membantu)
👑 Irregular Verbs (Kata Kerja Tak Beraturan):
• go ➔ went (pergi)
• eat ➔ ate (makan)
• drink ➔ drank (minum)
• see ➔ saw (melihat)
• buy ➔ bought (membeli)
• take ➔ took (mengambil)
• find ➔ found (menemukan)
• swim ➔ swam (berenang)
• feel ➔ felt (merasa)
